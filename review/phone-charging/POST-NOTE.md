## Instagram caption
The last part of your phone's charge is slow on purpose. 🔋
How phone charging works: a lithium-ion battery charges in two stages. Stage 1 (constant current) pushes a steady current while the voltage climbs; with fast charging it can reach about 70% quickly. When each cell hits its limit, about 4.2 V, the battery is only around 85% full. Stage 2 (constant voltage) holds that limit while the current shrinks, so the last few percent creep in.
Why not push harder? Charging above the voltage limit stresses the cell and can plate metallic lithium, and high voltage is where a battery ages fastest. The slow finish isn't your charger failing; it's the battery being protected.
