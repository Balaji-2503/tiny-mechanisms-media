## Instagram caption
This tiny wire is built to destroy itself. ⚡ One mechanism a day.
A fuse is a thin strip of metal in the circuit. Current heats it, and heat grows with the square of the current: double the current, four times the heat. When something goes wrong the thin strip melts first, the circuit opens and the wires are safe. A breaker does the same job and can be reset: a bimetal strip bends on slow overloads and an electromagnet snaps it open on a surge.
