## Instagram caption
The internet between continents doesn't come from space. 🌊 It runs through cables on the sea floor, most about as wide as a garden hose.
How undersea internet cables work: glass fibres about as thin as a hair carry your data as pulses of laser light, wrapped in plastic and sometimes steel wire. There are 600+ active and planned cables, over 1.5 million km in service; satellites carry only a very small share.
About 200 faults happen every year, two-thirds from fishing gear and ship anchors, and fish bites (sharks included) caused zero from 2007 to 2014. Just last week a fault on one cable off Pakistan's coast slowed the country's internet until traffic was rerouted.
