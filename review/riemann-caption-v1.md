First 10 trillion zeros checked. All on one line. Still no proof.

The Riemann hypothesis (167 years old, a $1 million Millennium Prize problem) says every non-obvious zero of the zeta function sits on this line. It is still unproven. In 2026 OpenAI reported a weaker result that fences the zeros into a narrower strip, between 1/12 and 11/12. Quanta reports that experts largely agree it looks correct; it is not yet formally verified, and it is not the Riemann hypothesis itself.
