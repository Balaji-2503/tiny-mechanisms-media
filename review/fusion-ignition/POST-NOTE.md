## Instagram caption
In 2022, a fusion experiment got out more energy than its lasers put in. 🔥 But there's a catch.
How fusion ignition works: up to 192 laser beams fire into a hollow cylinder about 1 cm long. The light becomes X-rays that blast the surface of a peppercorn-sized fuel capsule; it implodes like a rocket in reverse, squeezing hydrogen fuel to star-core conditions until deuterium and tritium fuse into helium. On 5 Dec 2022: 2.05 MJ of laser light in, 3.15 MJ of fusion energy out. It has been repeated 12 times since, with a record of 8.6 MJ.
The catch: ignition only counts the laser energy that reaches the target. Running those lasers took about 300 MJ from the grid, roughly 100× more than the fusion output. A real scientific milestone, not a power plant yet.
