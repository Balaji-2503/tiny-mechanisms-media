## Instagram caption
Why does only ONE key open this lock? 🔑
Inside a pin tumbler lock, springs push pairs of pins down. The right key lifts every pin to exactly the same line (the shear line), and only then can the plug turn. A key that is right for four pins out of five stays stuck. Example: 5 pins x 6 heights = 7,776 possible keys (an illustration, not a real lock model).
