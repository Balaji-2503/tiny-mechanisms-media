## Instagram caption
The crystal in your watch vibrates 32,768 times every second, and that strange number was chosen on purpose. ⌚
How a quartz watch works: quartz is piezoelectric (bend it and it makes a charge; put a voltage on it and it bends), so the circuit keeps a tiny quartz tuning fork ringing at a steady rate, trimmed to 32,768 Hz.
Why 32,768? It's 2¹⁵. A flip-flop circuit halves a frequency, so 15 of them in a row turn 32,768 into exactly one pulse per second, which drives a tiny stepping motor that ticks the second hand. It's also just above human hearing and low enough to save battery.
