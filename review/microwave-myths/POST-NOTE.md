## Instagram caption
Your microwave does NOT cook food from the inside out. 🍲 That's a myth.
How a microwave works: the 2.45 GHz waves make water molecules twist, and that motion becomes heat, but in most foods they only reach about 1 cm. The middle warms later, by heat creeping inward. Second microwave myth: 2.45 GHz isn't water's special frequency (water absorbs more strongly at higher frequencies, around 10 GHz and up, so those waves would stop at the surface); the lower frequency simply reaches deeper.
3 microwave cooking tips that fix cold spots: 1️⃣ put food off centre on the turntable 2️⃣ use low power or defrost for thick or frozen food 3️⃣ let it sit a minute.
