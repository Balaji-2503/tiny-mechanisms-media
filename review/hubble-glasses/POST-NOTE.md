## Instagram caption
NASA launched a telescope that couldn't focus. 🔭 Its 2.4 m mirror was too flat at the edge by about 1/50 of a human hair.
Hubble's mirror flaw explained: a perfect mirror sends every ray of light to one focus point; Hubble's edge sent light somewhere else, so it had many focus points and fuzzy images (spherical aberration). The cause was the tool used to test the mirror: one lens inside it was spaced 1.3 mm wrong, so the mirror was shaped to a wrong reference.
The fix: in December 1993 astronauts installed a camera with its own correcting optics and COSTAR, a fridge-sized box of small mirrors, Hubble's "glasses". The main mirror still has the same flaw today; every later instrument carries the fix.
