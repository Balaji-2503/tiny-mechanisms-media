## Instagram caption
Engineers saved the Leaning Tower of Pisa by digging under it. 🏗️ Not under the side it leans toward, under the other side.
Why it leans: building began in 1173, and by 1178, at the second floor, it was already sinking. It stood on a foundation only 3 m deep, in soft ground that could not carry its weight evenly.
By 1990 the lean reached 5.5° and the tower was closed. First, 870 tonnes of lead went on the high side as a counterweight. Then the clever part: engineers slowly removed 38 m³ of soil from under the raised end. As the ground settled there, the tower leaned back to about 4°, where it was in 1838. In 2008 it was announced that it had stopped moving for the first time in its history.
They never wanted it straight. Just safe.
