## Instagram caption
Your phone's GPS has to correct for Einstein. 🛰️ Without relativity, your position would drift by about 10 km every day.
How GPS works: satellites about 20,000 km up broadcast the time from atomic clocks, and your phone times each signal. Light covers about 30 cm in a billionth of a second, so tiny timing errors become big distance errors.
Special relativity: the satellites move fast, so their clocks tick slower (about −7 µs a day). General relativity: gravity is weaker up there, so they tick faster (about +45 µs a day). Net: about +38 µs a day. The fix: the satellite clocks are set to tick slightly slower before launch.
